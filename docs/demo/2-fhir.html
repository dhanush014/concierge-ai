Why FHIR

  • It's what hospitals expose. US rules require certified EHRs (Epic, Oracle Health) to offer FHIR APIs.
  • Swap the source, keep the pipeline. Synthea files today, a hospital's FHIR API tomorrow.
  • Why Synthea: realistic lifelong histories with labs, meds, visits and insurance, and zero privacy risk.

"When is my next visit?"

"What was my last A1c?" / "my meds?"

"Is a ride to my visit covered?"

Insurance plan chunks for this patient's insurer only → check_my_coverage
