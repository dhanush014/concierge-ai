Concierge AI

An assistant in the patient portal that answers from your own record and insurance, and hands anything clinical to a nurse.

The problem

  • Portal messages keep growing, and nurses read every one by hand.
  • Most are routine: "When is my visit?" "What's my copay?" "What was my A1c?"
  • Patients can't decode their own insurance plan.
  • A generic chatbot answers everything, even what it shouldn't: chest pain, "do I have cancer?"

What makes Concierge different

  • Safety check before the AI. Every message: emergency → 911, clinical → nurse.
  • Answers only from your data. Your record, your insurer's plan, with sources.
  • AI suggests, code acts. It finds the slot; you press Confirm.
  • A real nurse handoff. The agent pauses, the nurse chats live, and then it picks up again.
  • Measured, not guessed. Every chat is traced, nurses grade it, and evals gate every release.
