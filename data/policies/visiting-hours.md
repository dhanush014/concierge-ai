# Visiting hours

## General wards

Visiting hours on general wards are 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, every day. Up to 3 visitors may be at the bedside at one time.

## Intensive care unit (ICU)

ICU visiting is 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Only 2 visitors at a time, and both must be 16 or older. Please call the ICU desk at extension 4410 before visiting.

## Maternity

One support person may stay overnight in a maternity room. Other visitors are welcome from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM; siblings of the newborn may visit at any time during those hours.

## Children's ward

Parents and guardians may visit at any time. Other visitors follow general ward hours.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours are 9:00 PM to 7:00 AM on all wards. Please keep phone calls short and use headphones.

## When to stay home

Do not visit if you have a fever, cough, vomiting, or diarrhea in the last 48 hours. Masks are available at every entrance.
