# Medical records requests

## Online access

Your lab results, visit notes, medications, and immunizations are available free of charge in the patient portal.

## Requesting a full copy

To request a complete copy of your record, fill in the records request form in the portal or at the Health Information desk (Main Clinic, Suite 110). Requests are completed within 15 business days.

## Fees

Electronic copies sent by secure email or portal download are free. Paper copies cost $0.25 per page, capped at $25 per request. Copies sent directly to another doctor for your care are always free.

## Sending records to someone else

To send your records to another person or organization, sign the authorization form. You can cancel an authorization in writing at any time.

## Corrections

If you think something in your record is wrong, submit a correction request. We reply within 30 days.

## Records for a child or another adult

Parents and legal guardians can request records for a child under 18. For another adult, we need a signed authorization or a healthcare power of attorney.
