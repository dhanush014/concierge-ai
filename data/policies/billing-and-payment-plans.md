# Billing and payment plans

## Statements

Riverside Health sends one statement per month, after your insurance has processed the claim. Payment is due 30 days after the statement date. You can pay in the patient portal, by phone at 555-0142, or at the cashier desk in the Main Clinic lobby.

## Payment plans

If you cannot pay a balance in full, you can set up an interest-free payment plan for balances of $200 or more. Monthly payments start at $25, and a plan can last up to 24 months. There is no fee to set up a plan. Call billing or start a plan in the portal before the due date.

## Financial assistance

Patients with a household income at or below 250% of the federal poverty level may qualify for a full or partial discount. Apply with the financial assistance form in the portal or at the cashier desk; a decision is made within 30 days. Collections are paused while an application is reviewed.

## Price estimates

You can ask for a written estimate before a planned service. Estimates are provided within 3 business days.

## Questions about a bill

Call billing at 555-0142, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Have your statement number ready.
