# Lab and fasting prep

Riverside Health Laboratory, Main Clinic, ground floor (Suite 104).

## Lab hours

The lab is open Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 3:30 PM, and Saturday 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Walk-ins are welcome; booking through the patient portal saves an average of 20 minutes of waiting.

## Fasting blood work

Fast for 8 to 12 hours before a lipid panel (cholesterol), a fasting glucose test, or a comprehensive metabolic panel. During the fast you may drink plain water. Do not drink coffee, tea, juice, or alcohol, and do not chew gum. If your appointment is before 9:00 AM, finish your last meal by 9:00 PM the night before.

## Medicines before a blood test

Keep taking your usual medicines unless your care team told you otherwise. Bring a list of everything you take, including vitamins and supplements. If you take insulin or another diabetes medicine, ask your care team how to adjust it before fasting.

## Tests that do not need fasting

A1c (hemoglobin A1c), complete blood count (CBC), thyroid tests (TSH), and most vitamin levels do not require fasting.

## Urine samples

For a urine test, collect the first urine of the morning in the sterile cup from the lab. Bring it within 2 hours and keep it in the refrigerator until you leave.

## Results

Most results appear in the patient portal within 2 business days. Some specialized tests take up to 10 business days. Your care team contacts you directly about any result that needs follow-up.
