# Parking

## Main Clinic garage

The Main Clinic garage on Elm Street is open 24 hours. The first 30 minutes are free. After that, parking costs $2 per hour, up to $12 per day.

## Validation

Patients get 3 hours of free parking with validation. Validate your ticket at any reception desk before you leave.

## Accessible parking

Accessible spaces are on level 1, next to the elevators. Free wheelchairs are available at the garage entrance.

## Weekly passes

Visitors of inpatients can buy a weekly pass for $30 at the cashier desk.

## Drop-off

The drop-off zone at the Main Clinic entrance allows stops of up to 10 minutes. Valet parking is available Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, for $8.

## Electric vehicles

Eight EV chargers are on level 2. Charging is free for the first 2 hours.
