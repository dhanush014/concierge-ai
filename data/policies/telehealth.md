# Telehealth visits

## Joining a visit

Join your video visit from the Appointments tab, 10 minutes before the start time, to test your camera and microphone. The link opens in your browser; no app is needed.

## Supported devices

Use a recent version of Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox on a computer, phone, or tablet. You need a camera, a microphone, and an internet connection of at least 5 Mbps.

## If the video does not work

If the video link fails, stay where you are: your clinician calls the phone number on your profile within 10 minutes and finishes the visit by phone.

## What telehealth is for

Telehealth works well for follow-ups, medication reviews, and going over lab results. It is not for emergencies, and some problems need an in-person exam.

## Privacy

Join from a private place. Visits are not recorded.

## State rules

Your clinician must be licensed in the state you are in during the visit. Tell us if you will be traveling.
