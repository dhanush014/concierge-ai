# Imaging prep

Riverside Health Imaging, North Campus, Building B.

## Check-in

Arrive 30 minutes before your scan to check in and change. Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and the referral if you have a paper one.

## MRI

Remove all metal before an MRI: jewelry, hairpins, watches, and clothing with zippers or metal threads. Tell the technologist if you have a pacemaker, cochlear implant, insulin pump, or any metal in your body; some devices are not safe in the scanner. An MRI usually takes 30 to 60 minutes. Earplugs are provided because the scanner is loud.

## CT scan with contrast

Do not eat for 4 hours before a CT scan with contrast. You may drink clear liquids up to 1 hour before. If you have kidney disease or diabetes, or are allergic to iodine contrast, tell your care team when you book. Drink extra water for the rest of the day after the scan.

## Ultrasound

For an abdominal ultrasound, do not eat or drink for 6 hours before. For a pelvic ultrasound, drink 32 ounces (about 1 liter) of water 1 hour before and do not empty your bladder.

## X-ray

No preparation is needed for most X-rays. Wear clothes without metal or be ready to change into a gown.

## Pregnancy

Tell the technologist if you are or might be pregnant before any X-ray or CT scan.
