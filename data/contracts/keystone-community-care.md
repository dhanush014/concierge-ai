# Keystone Community Care — Keystone Medicaid Choice

Fictional plan summary for the Riverside Health demo. Not a real insurer, not a real Medicaid plan, and not real coverage terms.

## Copays

Primary care visit: $0. Specialist visit: $3. Urgent care: $0. Emergency room: $0 for emergencies. Telehealth visit: $0. Prescriptions: $1 generic, $3 brand name.

## Deductible and out-of-pocket maximum

There is no deductible. Members pay only the small copays above.

## Prior authorization

These services need prior authorization: MRI and CT scans, non-emergency ambulance rides, and medical equipment. Primary care, lab work and family planning services do not need prior authorization.

## Extra benefits

Free rides to medical appointments: book at least 2 business days ahead. Adult dental cleanings twice a year are covered.

## Not covered

Cosmetic surgery and experimental treatments are not covered.

## Appeals

You can appeal a denied service within 60 days of the notice.
