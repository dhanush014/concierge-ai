# Allegheny Shield — Allegheny Shield Blue PPO

Fictional plan summary for the Riverside Health demo. Not a real insurer or real coverage terms.

## Copays

Primary care visit: $30. Specialist visit: $60. Urgent care: $50. Emergency room: $250. Telehealth visit: $15.

## Deductible and out-of-pocket maximum

The deductible is $2,000 per person per year. The out-of-pocket maximum is $7,000 per person per year.

## Prior authorization

These services need prior authorization: MRI and PET scans, bariatric surgery, inpatient rehabilitation, and home sleep tests. CT scans do not need prior authorization on this plan.

## Not covered

Cosmetic surgery, adult vision exams, and over-the-counter medicines are not covered.

## Appeals

You can appeal a denied claim within 180 days of the denial letter.
