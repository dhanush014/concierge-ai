# Summit Health Partners — Summit PPO 1500

Fictional plan summary for the Riverside Health demo. Not a real insurer or real coverage terms.

## Copays

Primary care visit: $25. Specialist visit: $45, no referral needed. Urgent care: $75. Emergency room: $350. Telehealth visit: $10.

## Deductible and out-of-pocket maximum

The in-network deductible is $1,500 per person per year. The out-of-pocket maximum is $6,000 per person per year. Out-of-network care has a separate $3,000 deductible.

## Prior authorization

These services need prior authorization: MRI and CT scans, outpatient surgery, specialty drugs, and genetic testing. Routine lab work does not need prior authorization.

## Not covered

Cosmetic surgery, infertility treatment, and acupuncture are not covered.

## Appeals

You can appeal a denied claim within 180 days of the denial letter.
