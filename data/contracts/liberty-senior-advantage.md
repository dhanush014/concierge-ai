# Liberty Senior Advantage — Liberty Senior Advantage Plus

Fictional plan summary for the Riverside Health demo. Not a real insurer, not a real Medicare plan, and not real coverage terms.

## Copays

Primary care visit: $0. Specialist visit: $35. Urgent care: $40. Emergency room: $110, waived if you are admitted within 24 hours. Telehealth visit: $0.

## Deductible and out-of-pocket maximum

There is no deductible for medical services. The out-of-pocket maximum is $4,900 per year.

## Prior authorization

These services need prior authorization: MRI and CT scans, home health care, durable medical equipment over $500 (such as wheelchairs), and skilled nursing facility stays. Annual wellness visits and flu shots do not need prior authorization.

## Extra benefits

The plan covers one routine eye exam and one routine hearing exam per year, a $150 yearly allowance toward glasses, and a fitness program membership at no cost.

## Not covered

Cosmetic surgery, long-term custodial care, and care received outside the United States (except emergencies) are not covered.

## Appeals

You can appeal a denied claim within 65 days of the denial notice.
