# Riverbend Health — Riverbend Silver HMO

Fictional plan summary for the Riverside Health demo. Not a real insurer or real coverage terms.

## Copays

Primary care visit: $20. Specialist visit: $50, with a referral from your primary care doctor. Urgent care: $60. Emergency room: $300, waived if you are admitted. Telehealth visit: $0.

## Deductible and out-of-pocket maximum

The deductible is $1,000 per person per year. Copays do not count toward the deductible. The out-of-pocket maximum is $5,500 per person per year.

## Referrals

As an HMO, Riverbend Silver needs a referral from your primary care doctor before you see a specialist. Without a referral, the specialist visit is not covered.

## Prior authorization

These services need prior authorization from Riverbend before they happen: MRI and CT scans, planned hospital stays, physical therapy after the first 6 visits, and sleep studies. Lab work and X-rays do not need prior authorization.

## Not covered

Cosmetic surgery, adult dental care, hearing aids, and weight-loss programs are not covered.

## Appeals

You can appeal a denied claim within 180 days of the denial letter.
